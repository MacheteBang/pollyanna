Three directions for Pollyanna's new look

The same two screens in each: home, and the event page with the reveal. Tap the reveal to untie it. The bow and girl are rough stand-ins to show each style, not final drawings. Switch with the bar below or the arrow keys.

A · One ribbon

One hot color, ink, and a single line of cord. Your events hang on the cord in date order; at the reveal you pull the bow out and the ribbon lies flat over the name.

The one bold thing
The cord. Orange marks only what is waiting on you, and the gift itself.
Paper metaphor
Gone. Flat color, square corners, no texture or tilt.
Type
Figtree for everything, heavy for names and actions.
Main risk
The nearest to an ordinary app. It leans on the orange, the cord and the wordmark to be its own thing.

B · Ink and bows

A cool white page, a loose ink line, and color only inside the bows. The girl stands at the top of the page looking in at your exchanges; the name arrives in handwriting.

The one bold thing
The drawing. Everything else is type on a bare page with no boxes.
Paper metaphor
A trace of it: ink on a page, and the reveal is a folded note. No kraft, stamps, tears or tilt.
Type
Young Serif for names and headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading, Shantell Sans for the revealed name only.
Main risk
It needs a real hand. The wobble here is a filter standing in for one, and a weak line looks worse than none. A serif with a warm accent can also drift toward the look §12 bans, so the page stays cool and the bows stay raspberry.

C · Cut shapes

Three flat colors and big rounded blocks. The bow is two wedges and a dot; the girl is built from the same few shapes. Each event is a block you could pick up, and the gift is the biggest block of all.

The one bold thing
Scale: huge names, huge countdown numbers, a bow the size of your thumb.
Paper metaphor
Gone as a material, though flat cut shapes still feel a little like craft paper.
Type
Bricolage Grotesque for everything, very heavy at large sizes.
Main risk
The busiest of the three, and it brings back a stack of cards. Bright rounded shapes are widely copied, and three colors can quickly become five.
