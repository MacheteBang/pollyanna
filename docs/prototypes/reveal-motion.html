How the reveal looks and moves

Three ways the bow could come undone on the event page, in the tablecloth direction. Tap the gift or the button to untie it. Switch variants with the bar at the bottom or the left and right arrow keys.

State: Tied

What moves, in order

BeatStarts, lasts

The same in all three

  • Tied, the bow is the kept mark, unchanged. Untying pulls the tails longer while the loops shrink into the knot, then the knot lets go and the bow drops away.
  • The name is set in the hand (Playpen Sans), 52px, or 35px when it is long.
  • Every visit starts tied, so a phone left on the table gives nothing away. "Hide the name" ties it back at once, with no motion.
  • With reduce motion on, the name and notes simply replace the gift.
  • Nothing else on the page moves.
