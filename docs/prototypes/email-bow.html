A mail client that darkens an email leaves the picture alone and darkens the ground behind it. Drawn as it is today, the bow's ink knot sinks into that ground and the two loops come apart. Below is the bow four ways, each on white and darkened, in the email and close up.

Four picks. The page opens on what I'd choose.

  1. Which image. A white edge (recommended): the mark stays as drawn and matches the reveal and the touch icon. Or another knot, a white tile, or the bow left as drawn.
  2. Size beside the wordmark: 26px recommended.
  3. Folds at that size: kept (recommended) or left out, as on the favicon.
  4. Alt text: none (recommended), since the wordmark right beside it already says the name, or "Pollyanna".
