Organizing an event

  • Top to bottom: the two-way switch, the title, the row of steps, then the one next step on the cloth. Whatever blocks that step replaces the sentence and the button.
  • People are a plain list. A row only says something when something is missing.
  • "Who can't draw whom" and "Details and organizers" are closed rows with a summary on the right. Tap one to open it in place.
  • "Bigger changes" is always open at the bottom: real outlined buttons, each with one line on what it does. Only "Cancel this event" is red.
  • Once revealed, the full "who drew whom" list takes the place of the people list.

Choices for you

  1. Empty home: A (one panel, two halves) or B (two panels). I'd pick A: it keeps one panel per screen, and the dividing line makes the two ways in read as a pair.
  2. The girl on error and sign-in failure pages: shown or left out. I'd show her; it's the only thing on those pages that is Pollyanna's.
  3. Red on "Delete my account": the system says red is for errors and "Cancel this event" only. I'd extend it to this one button, since it's the same kind of act, and to nothing else. "Leave this event" stays ink.
  4. No cloth on confirmations (leave, delete account), and none on questions when nothing is new. Cloth appears on: home, the event page, organizing, a new question, new event, sign-in and signed out.
  5. "Bigger changes" as the name of the rare-and-risky group on the organizing page, always open at the bottom rather than folded shut.
  6. Sign-in with several providers: every provider button outlined, none filled.
